# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss technical evidence and decisions without harassment, discrimination, threats or disclosure of another person's private information. Disagreement is welcome when it remains specific and constructive.

Do not post credentials, confidential datasets or sensitive experiment records in public discussions or issue attachments.

Report conduct concerns privately to protoscience@anulum.li. The maintainer may remove harmful material, restrict participation or decline a contribution to protect participants. Reports are handled with appropriate discretion; no unsupported promise of absolute confidentiality is made.
