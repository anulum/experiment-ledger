# Security policy

Send security-sensitive reports to protoscience@anulum.li. Describe the affected source revision, expected and observed behaviour, a minimal non-sensitive reproducer and the relevant environment. Do not include credentials, private signing material or confidential research data.

The project currently has no released Ledger runtime or hosted service. Its repository foundation is being developed. There is no supported production-version matrix, certification or device-durability claim.

Maintainers assess reports against the actual affected surface and coordinate disclosure where appropriate. Do not test against another person's infrastructure or data without permission. See [the threat model](docs/THREAT_MODEL.md) for the intended trust boundaries and current assurance limits.
